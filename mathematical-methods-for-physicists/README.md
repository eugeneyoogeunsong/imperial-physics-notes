## Topics

- Vector spaces, tensors, and coordinate transformations
- Sturm–Liouville theory and eigenfunction expansions
- Green's functions, in one dimension and in higher dimensions
- Integral transforms: Fourier and Laplace
- Complex analysis: analyticity and the Cauchy–Riemann conditions, Cauchy's theorem, residues, contour integration and principal values
- Variational methods, including the variational principle for energy eigenvalues
