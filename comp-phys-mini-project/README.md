## Quantum Waves in Motion

A finite-difference time-domain solver for the time-dependent Schrödinger
equation, built in Mathematica, with animations of wave-packet dispersion,
reflection and tunnelling. Awarded the highest grade.

- `quantum-waves-in-motion/report.pdf` - the write-up.
- `quantum-waves-in-motion/tdse-simulator.nb` - the Mathematica notebook.
- `quantum-waves-in-motion/videos/` - animations for four potentials:
  free particle, step well, finite square well, quadratic well.

Open the notebook in Mathematica and evaluate top to bottom; the animations
regenerate from the solver rather than being pre-baked.
