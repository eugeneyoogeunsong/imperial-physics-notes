## Topics

- Lagrangian mechanics: the action principle, Fermat's principle, constraints and equilibrium
- Small oscillations and normal modes
- Hamiltonian mechanics: Poisson brackets and canonical transformations
- Rigid-body dynamics and rotating frames
- Symmetries, Noether's theorem, and active versus passive transformations
- Classical field theory, and Maxwell's equations as a U(1) gauge theory
- Gauge symmetry, and the route from there to the electroweak SU(2) × U(1)
